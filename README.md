# ParkMark

A lightweight SwiftUI iOS app (with WidgetKit) for quickly tracking which floor you parked on—across multiple customizable car parks—and viewing that info at a glance via home-screen widgets.

---

## Features

- **Multiple Car Parks**
  Create, edit, and delete any number of car parks.

- **Custom Floor Labels**
  Choose alphabetical (A–Z), numeric (1–12), or a custom list (e.g. B2, G, 1).

- **Quick Save**
  Tap your floor button and hit “Save” to store the exact floor and timestamp.

- **Home-Screen Widgets**
  Small and medium widgets show your active park and last saved floor without opening the app.

---

## Quick Start

# 1. Clone the repository
```bash
git clone https://github.com/your-username/ParkMark.git
cd ParkMark
```

# 2. Open in Xcode 15+ (iOS 17+)
open `ParkMark.xcodeproj`

# 3. Build and Run on Simulator or Device
* Select your target (e.g. iPhone 15 simulator)

* Press ⌘R to install and launch

* Tap the “+” in the header to add a new car park.

* Choose a name and floor scheme (alphabetical, numeric, or custom).

* Select your floor, then tap Save Floor.

* To add a widget: long-press your Home Screen → tap “+” → search for ParkMark → choose Small or Medium.

## Want This on Your Phone?

To install ParkMark directly on your own iPhone for testing:

* Connect your iPhone to your Mac via USB.
* Open `ParkMark.xcodeproj` in Xcode.
* Select your iPhone from the device list (top toolbar).
* Trust the Developer Profile on your phone if prompted:
* On your iPhone: Settings → General → Device Management → trust your Apple ID.
* Press `⌘R` in Xcode to build and run.
* ParkMark will install on your iPhone, and you can launch it like any other app.
